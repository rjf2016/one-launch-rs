**Goal.** The other three adapters and their simulators in parallel, the remaining services, every command, and full replay parity. The UI kit track finishes alongside.

`E\` is the Electron repo at the pin.

## Tickets

Three launcher lanes in parallel:

| Lane | Tickets | Ports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epic | E1 to E5, SIM2 | `E\src\main\launchers\epic\adapter.ts` (1,483 lines), `sim\epic.ts` |
