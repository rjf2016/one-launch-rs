# M3 Steam slice

**Goal.** One launcher end to end, through every layer: adapter, simulator, launch service, command surface, replay, and Play through the UI. Steam is the smallest adapter, so it validates the adapter trait, `SwitchCtx`, the backup interface, the operation log and the replay runner before three larger adapters are ported in parallel.

`E\` is the Electron repo at the pin.

## Exit gate

- Steam replays match: play, already-active, stop, rollback, wrong-account, elevated.
- Play works in a real window against the simulator, driven through the automation pipe.
- The adapter trait is frozen.
- A performance ratchet is in CI from here on.
