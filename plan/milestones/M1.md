## Spike rules

- **Where.** Code in `spikes/sNN/`, a crate F1 creates empty with its dependencies declared. The record in `../decisions/M1-S<n>.md`: the decision, the numbers, how to repeat the run, what the product takes from it, and which sentences of `../design/*.md` it confirms or corrects. A pattern others will copy goes to `docs/cookbook/`, one file per pattern.
- **Proof, not product.** Nothing from a spike moves into a crate without a ticket. A spike may use `unsafe`; the product keeps it in `ol-platform-win`.
- **Facts.** A GPUI fact comes from the pinned source in the cargo registry, cited by file. `gpui-notes.md` is unverified until a record confirms it. The spike does not edit the design files: its record lists the corrections, and the retro at the gate applies them.
- **Safety.** The standing rules hold in full: no launcher, no real data folder, no installer on the owner's PC, no process tool, the Riot rules. A spike measures its own process, or a child it started, through the handle it got from starting it.
- **Dependencies.** Only what F1 resolved. A spike that needs more stops and asks for a `deps` ticket.
- **Builds.** One GPUI build at a time on the owner's PC.
- **What an agent cannot prove.** A check that needs a person (an Explorer restart, a session end, an IME, the snap flyout) is the ticket's `owner_step`, queued for the gate. The spike still ends with everything else proven, and its record says which lines wait.
- **Workflow files.** A spike that needs a CI job commits the workflow file and stops; the owner pushes it (`../design/workflow.md` › "Acting as the app").

## Exit gate

- One decision record per spike.
- The skeleton screenshot is produced and compared by CI.
- The guardrails reject a deliberately bad PR of each kind: out-of-scope edit, removed test, protected-path edit, banned crate.
- Cost per merged ticket measured on the first tickets.

## The owner's part

- GPUI go/no-go from the spike sheet.
- Read the one-page architecture (crate graph, traits).
- Install anything that needs elevation.
- Add the required check `gate` to the `main` ruleset once CI reports it.
- Push each workflow commit an agent leaves waiting, and approve pull requests on owner-approved paths.
- The steps queued by the spikes: S1 (Explorer restart, sign-out), S2 (snap flyout, Win+Z, drag to snap), S4 (an IME, if one is installed), S10 (approve the registry file into the oracle), S12 (look at the effects that have no equivalent).
