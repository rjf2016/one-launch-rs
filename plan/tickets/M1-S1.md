## Outcome

`plan/decisions/M1-S1.md`: pass, or the fallback named in `plan/milestones/M1.md` (a helper window of our own with `Shell_NotifyIconW`; destroy and recreate the main window if GPUI cannot hide one and keep its state). The record says which mechanism holds each of the six checks, and what `ol-platform-win` and `ol-app` take from it. `docs/cookbook/tray.md` holds the pattern.

Try the direct route first: a message-only helper window and `Shell_NotifyIconW` through the `windows` crate. It adds no dependency. `tray-icon` and `muda` are approved only if this spike picks them; picking them is a `deps` ticket, so say why the direct route was not enough.

## Out of scope

The tray menu's final items and icons (Milestone 5). Single instance (Milestone 5). Start with Windows itself.

## Traps

- `set_quit_mode` is from the notes, unverified: find it in the pinned source before relying on it.
- A hidden GPUI window may still tick. The zero-frame check is the one that matters for idle CPU.
- Foreground rules: Windows may refuse `SetForegroundWindow` unless the call follows the user's click on the icon. A simulated callback can pass where a real click fails, and the reverse. Say which was tried.
- An agent never restarts Explorer or ends a session: both are the owner's steps, and process tools are forbidden.
