## Outcome

`plan/decisions/M1-S3.md`: pass, or the fallback (a thumbnail cache on disk, which needs a triage row because it adds files to the data folder). The record gives the numbers, the virtualisation and decode design that reached them, and the cache size that keeps memory under the line. `docs/cookbook/poster-grid.md` holds the pattern.

The posters are generated by the spike into a scratch folder: 500 distinct images at the size the artwork cache stores. No real artwork, no network.

## Out of scope

Card content beyond the poster and a title, hover states, the Recent strip, sorting and filtering.

## Traps

- The grid is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(160px, 1fr))` with gap 16, and 190 with gap 20 from 1536 px (`LibraryView.tsx`). Compute columns from that rule, not from the sidecar.
- Full-size posters for a few hundred cards cost hundreds of megabytes. Decode at display size, keep a bounded cache, and say how eviction behaves on a fast scroll.
- Measure frames while scrolling with input, not with an idle window.
- The working set is read from the spike's own process. Process tools are forbidden.
- Frame times on this PC flatter the result; say what hardware the numbers are from and repeat the run on the CI runner once S9 passes.
