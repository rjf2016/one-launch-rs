## Outcome

`plan/decisions/M1-S6.md`: pass, or stop: without this the takeover design changes and the owner decides how. The record gives the exact key derivation and blob layout that worked, what Rust must preserve in `Local State`, whether two apps on one folder need a lock, and whether the WAL files of one app are accepted by the other. What `ol-vault` and `ol-store` take from it is listed.

## Out of scope

The real `ol-vault` and `ol-store`. A CI job for the cross-read (Milestone 2).

## Traps

- Scratch folders only. `%APPDATA%\OneLaunch` holds the owner's secrets and is never opened, listed or named in a command.
- DPAPI binds to this Windows user. Both halves of a cross-read run in one session on one machine; a golden file cannot hold a DPAPI blob.
- Chromium writes `Local State` some seconds after first use (spec 4.4). A folder read too early has no key yet.
- The Electron repo is read-only. Run its dev build from the oracle tooling branch; change nothing there.
- Print counts and booleans. Never a key, a plaintext or a blob, in a log, a record or a commit.
- DPAPI is a Win32 call: the spike may use `unsafe`; the product keeps it in `ol-platform-win`.
