## Outcome

A headless vitest harness that composes the real adapters, simulators, services and IPC handlers with `electron` mocked and the fake vault, in the same order `src/main/index.ts` wires them, recording at the mocked `ipcMain.handle`.

A small state-gated script language (`invoke`, `awaitJob ... until`, `crash`, `restart`, `snapshot`) and 22 scripts in `oracle/l2/scripts/`: the 20 headless scenarios plus the two partial ones (`quit-during-switch` shutdown op, `first-run` empty-platform reads).

For each script, `oracle/l2/expected/<name>.json`: the event stream (job transitions and platform operations with one sequence), and the final state (normalised DB dump, journal, backups, launcher files as parsed projections, WARN and ERROR log lines).

## Out of scope

`update` (T06). UI-only, window and packaging scenarios. The four channels that call `shell` or native dialogs directly.

## Traps

- Scripts gate on state, never on time. `stop` and `handover` act when a step is reached.
- `shutdownPolls` counts `isRunning()` calls, so anything that adds polls (a renderer, a status poll) changes the trace. Keep the harness free of them.
- Simulator-stamped files differ per run; compare their parsed projection, not their bytes.
- Job stream comparison is a subsequence match; platform operations are exact order; final state is exact.
