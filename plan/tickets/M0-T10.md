## Outcome

`oracle/perf/baseline.json` and `METHOD.md`, measured on the packaged Electron build with a scratch data folder:

- warm start to interactive (median and p95 of at least 10);
- memory across all of the app's processes, visible and hidden to tray;
- idle CPU, visible and hidden, with the large fixture database;
- install size and installer size;
- `launchers:status` latency, read-only.

These are the numbers "lighter and faster" is judged against in Milestone 7 and ratcheted from Milestone 3.

## Out of scope

Switch-flow timings: fake mode is unpackaged-only, so there is no packaged baseline for them.

## Traps

- Compare warm starts only; first paint is GPU-bound and cold starts vary widely.
- Take the app's PIDs from the browser target's `SystemInfo.getProcessInfo`. Never match by image name; the installed copy may be in the tray.
- No process-listing tools (`tasklist`, `Get-Process`, WMI). Per-PID counters only, for PIDs the driver itself started.
