## Outcome

The states no agent may capture: rows 164 to 174 and 138 of `oracle/SCREENSHOTS.md`, plus real window chrome. Agents prepare the exact list with a file name per row and check what the owner provides against it.

`oracle/takeover/installed-registry.json` records what the shipped installer writes under the install key and the Uninstall key, as the ground truth for the takeover contract.

## Out of scope

Agents running an installer, opening the real data folder, or reading the registry of the owner's machine.

## Traps

- Screenshots of the real app can show account names. Capture on an empty or placeholder profile, or crop.
- Any of these the owner prefers to skip becomes a waiver row, not a silent gap.
