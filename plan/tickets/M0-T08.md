## Outcome

`oracle/assets/` holds:

- `copy.json`: every user-visible string from a TypeScript-compiler walk over `src/` (JSX text, literals, templates, with `file:line`), unioned with text observed in sidecars and replay step titles;
- `styles.json`: computed styles aggregated by tag and class signature from the sidecars, plus hover, focus and disabled states for about 12 primitives via `CSS.forcePseudoState`; the compiled renderer CSS alongside;
- `tokens.json`: colours, radii, shadows, fonts from `index.css`;
- `icons/`: the 54 Lucide icons as exact SVG, the app `.ico`, the tray PNGs, the brand mark;
- `whats-new.json`: the release entries;
- the licence text and what the installer's notices page must carry.

The static half (compiler walk, tokens, icons, What's New) can start before T07; the observed half follows it.

## Out of scope

Deciding wording. Strings are copied, never edited.

## Traps

- Template strings have slots; record the template and the slot names, not one rendered instance.
- Lucide SVG must come from the installed package version, rendered server-side, so stroke widths and sizes are exact.
- Tray PNGs come from a resize at runtime; reproduce the resize, do not copy the source image.
