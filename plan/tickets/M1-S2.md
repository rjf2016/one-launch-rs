## Outcome

`plan/decisions/M1-S2.md`: pass, or the fallback (the native title bar, tinted through DWM). The record gives the window options and the hit-test wiring that work, and what the baseline shows that the spike cannot match. `docs/cookbook/title-bar.md` holds the pattern.

## Out of scope

The title bar's content (brand mark, badge) and the sidebar. Window placement memory.

## Traps

- The snap flyout appears only when the maximise button answers hit-testing as the maximise button. A button drawn and handled by the app alone gets no flyout.
- A frameless window that maximises usually overhangs the screen by the frame width; check the fifth line on a real maximise, not by setting a size.
- The 960x600 minimum is in logical pixels. Check it at both scales.
- The baseline has the caption buttons as an OS overlay that the CDP shots lack (`architecture.md`). Compare against `oracle/ui/owner`, not against a shot.
- `WindowControlArea` is from the notes: confirm it in the pinned source.
