## Outcome

`plan/decisions/M1-S4.md`: pass, or the fallback (vendor Kit's input module into `ol-ui-kit`). The record lists which Kit types the input needs around it, what each costs in binary size, and how focus order and the modal trap are built in plain GPUI. Two cookbook entries.

## Out of scope

The look of the input (tokens, radius, sizes): Milestone 4's kit ticket. Every other Kit component.

## Traps

- Kit is approved for text input only. If the input cannot work without Kit's `Root` or `Theme`, that is the finding; do not adopt them quietly.
- The copy rule on a masked field is a safety matter, not polish: the field holds a password.
- IME cannot be proved by an agent. Do not claim it; queue the owner's step.
- Kit pins `gpui-pre` exactly. Use the workspace's versions and nothing newer.
