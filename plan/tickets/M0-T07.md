## Outcome

A capture kit on top of the existing driver, and scenario files `oracle-shots-<area>.mjs`, split across five agents by area: shell and Library; Accounts and account detail; Launch dialog and banner; Confirm, Recovery, Setup guide, What's New; Settings.

Per state, `oracle/ui/shots/<id>-<name>.png` and `.layout.json`: viewport, locale, timezone, focus, every text run (text, box, font, colour, volatile flag) and every interactive element (role, accessible name, ordinal, box, state, style).

About 145 states are automatic; 9 transient ones need a stall trick (1, 11, 20, 23, 62, 71, 111, 133, 143); the remaining buckets are in the design doc.

## Out of scope

Rows marked for the owner (T09). Hand-built style tables (T08 aggregates from the sidecars).

## Traps

- Set locale and timezone overrides; the UI formats relative dates with the system locale.
- Finish finite animations and pause infinite ones at t=0; wait for fonts and images before capturing.
- Point `TEMP` at a neutral folder: Settings shows the data path, and a PNG cannot be scanned for it.
- A covered window never paints. Take the shot before the first wait, or keep the window uncovered.
- Caption buttons are an OS overlay and never appear in these shots; that rectangle is masked in later comparisons.
- Fake launchers only. Clicking Play is allowed here and nowhere else.
